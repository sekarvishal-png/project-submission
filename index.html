About the IDEA Lab

The AICTE IDEA Lab at SRM Valliammai Engineering College is a shared space for students, faculty, and industry to turn ideas into working prototypes. It supports innovation, interdisciplinary learning, and research, and it encourages entrepreneurship among students.

Vision

To transform innovative ideas into real products that meet socio-economic needs, and to connect concept, development, and commercialization.

Objectives

  • Convert ideas into functional prototypes under one roof
  • Build creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills
  • Encourage multidisciplinary learning and teamwork
  • Make teaching and learning more engaging for engineering students

Training and Programs

  • Faculty development programs (FDPs)
  • Student skilling programs, ideation workshops, and internships
  • Industry workshops and professional skilling programs
  • Awareness programs for school and ITI students
  • Project support for students

Integrated Facilities

  • High Frequency System Lab
  • VLSI Lab
  • Robotics Lab
  • Communication Lab
  • AI & DS Lab
  • Centre of Excellence in Computing (IoT, Cyber Security)
